# Funding-rate signal analysis (Fase 3b)

> Generated by `python3 -m backtest.analyze_funding`. This document is the
> curated write-up; re-run the script to refresh the numbers.

## Data

- **Funding rates**: fetched live from the BloFin public
  `GET /api/v1/market/funding-rate-history` endpoint via the new
  `fetch_funding_history()` in `backtest/data_collector.py`, saved to
  `backtest/data/funding_btc_usdt.csv`. **1200 settlements**, 8-hour interval
  (3/day), covering **2025-04-07 → 2026-05-12**. (The pre-existing
  `funding_BTC-USDT.csv` from `funding_backfill.py` covered a slightly shorter
  window; the new file supersedes it and the analysis prefers it.)
- **BTC candles**: `backtest/data/BTC-USDT_1H.csv` (1H closes), spanning
  **2025-04-18 → 2026-04-18**. Forward returns are clipped to the end of the
  candle data, so the 24h/7d horizons drop the last few settlements and the
  effective overlap window is ~2025-04-19 → 2026-04-17 (**1166 settlements**).
- API was reachable; no fabricated data.

### No-lookahead construction

For a funding settlement at time `t` with rate `f`:
`close_t` = most recent candle close **at or before** `t`; `ret_h` =
`close(t+h) / close_t − 1` using the most recent close **at or before** `t+h`;
`regime` = the Fase-1 `classify_regimes()` label of the candle nearest `t`
(itself a per-bar, lookahead-free 30-day trailing-return rule). Funding is
known the instant it settles, so aligning a forward return that *starts* at `t`
to the rate observed *at* `t` introduces no lookahead.

## Funding-rate percentiles (per 8h settlement)

| pctile | rate | bps |
|---|---|---|
| p5  | −0.000114 | −1.14 |
| p25 | −0.000007 | −0.07 |
| p50 |  0.000087 |  0.88 |
| p75 |  0.000138 |  1.38 |
| p95 |  0.000179 |  1.79 |

Funding is **persistently mildly positive** (longs pay shorts) — the median is
+0.88 bps/8h (≈ +2.6 bps/day, ≈ +9.6%/yr carry). "Extreme" funding is roughly
**outside [p5, p95] = [−1.14 bps, +1.79 bps]**; the distribution is tight and
right-skewed, with negative funding being the rarer "extreme" tail.

## Correlation — funding vs. subsequent BTC return (Spearman)

| sample | 4h ρ (p) | 24h ρ (p) | 7d ρ (p) | 24h top-decile spread |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Full (n=1166)        | −0.011 (0.72) | −0.065 (0.031) | −0.130 (2e-5)  | −0.69% |
| In-sample / 1st half | +0.030 (0.48) | −0.113 (0.0065) | −0.240 (4e-9) | −0.71% |
| Out-of-sample / 2nd half | −0.064 (0.15) | −0.084 (0.058) | −0.177 (8e-5) | −0.57% |

Sign of the relationship is **negative**: high funding (crowded longs) → lower
subsequent BTC return — the textbook contrarian funding effect. But:

- **4h horizon: no signal** anywhere (|ρ| < 0.07, all p > 0.1).
- **24h horizon: marginal.** Significant in-sample (p=0.0065) but **fails
  out-of-sample** (p=0.058 — just misses the 5% bar). Effect size is borderline
  material (top-vs-bottom-decile 24h return spread ≈ −0.6%, just above the
  0.5% gate, and it shrinks OOS).
- **7d horizon: the only robust effect** — significant in-sample *and* OOS,
  |ρ| ≈ 0.18–0.24, decile spread ≈ −2.5% to −2.9%. But a 7-day forward horizon
  is far too slow for this bot's 5-minute / intraday trade cadence; it cannot
  be used as a per-trade risk input without rebuilding the strategy around it.

## Per-regime breakdown (full sample, 24h horizon)

| regime | n | 24h ρ (p) | 24h decile spread | 7d ρ (p) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| bull (n=379)     | 305 | **+0.038 (0.51)** — sign flips, not significant | +0.75% | +0.106 (0.066) |
| bear (n=306)     | 306 | −0.027 (0.63) — not significant | +0.57% | −0.212 (0.0002) |
| sideways (n=481) | 481 | **−0.176 (0.0001)** — significant | −0.91% | −0.268 (5e-9) |

The 24h funding effect is **not robust across regimes**: it is driven almost
entirely by the *sideways* regime. In *bull* markets the relationship actually
**reverses sign** (positive ρ), and in *bear* markets it is not significant.
A signal that only works in one of three regimes — and inverts in another —
is dangerous to wire into a position-size multiplier without regime gating.

## Decision gate (3b → 3c)

| criterion | result |
|---|---|
| significant in-sample (24h, p<0.05) | **yes** (p=0.0065) |

### VERDICT: **NO-GO** — do not integrate the funding rate into Fase 5's risk score.

**Reasoning.** On this ~1-year BTC-USDT sample the contrarian funding effect is
real in direction but (a) **fails the out-of-sample significance test at the
24h horizon** the plan targets, (b) is only **marginally** material there, and
(c) is **not robust across regimes** — it lives in the sideways regime and
*reverses* in bull markets. The only clean, OOS-stable signal sits at the **7d
horizon**, which is too slow to act on per trade. Per the IMPROVEMENT_PLAN.md
gate ("funding correlatie significant + materieel + robuust? Zo nee, funding
skippen in Fase 5"), funding is **dropped from the Fase 5 risk-score inputs**.

**Re-test triggers** (when it would be worth revisiting): a materially longer
funding history (multiple market cycles), or if Fase 6's bear-check explicitly
gates by regime — in which case the *sideways-only, 24h* funding tilt could be
reconsidered as a narrow, regime-conditioned component rather than a global
risk-score input. Not now.
